Welcome to Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh. It is famous for its mountains, rivers, snow and peaceful surroundings.

Places to Visit

Some popular places to visit in Manali are Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, Hidimba Temple and Mall Road.

Food in Manali

Manali offers many delicious foods such as momos, thukpa, siddu and traditional Himachali dishes.
